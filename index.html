🧠 How it works

Your text is processed through a machine learning pipeline trained on movie reviews.

01 Text Processing Convert review text into numerical features.
02 TF-IDF Features Represent words and phrases mathematically.
03 Classification Logistic Regression predicts sentiment.

💡 Research Note

This application uses the NLTK movie reviews dataset. Its predictions are intended for experimentation and educational demonstration. Results may vary for text outside the training domain.
